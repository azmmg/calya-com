Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7/26/2024

Introduction

Welcome to Calya.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

Data We Collect

We do not directly collect personal data from visitors to our website nor do we display third-party advertising.

Analytics Tools

Our website employs modern third-party analytics services to help us understand visitor behavior. This insight allows us to continually improve our online content and present relevant, engaging information to our users. The personal data collected by these third-party services is provided to us in an anonymized format, ensuring we cannot identify individual website visitors.

We utilize Google Analytics (GA4), a web analytics service offered by Google Inc. ("Google"). This service uses cookies to analyze website usage patterns. Typically, the data generated by these cookies about your website interaction is sent to and stored on Google's servers in the United States.

We have implemented Google Analytics with IP anonymization. This means your IP address is truncated within Switzerland, the European Union, or the European Economic Area before transmission. Only in rare instances is a full IP address sent to a Google server in the US and shortened there.

Google processes this information on our behalf to evaluate website usage, compile reports on website activity, and provide other services related to website and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties when required by law or when third parties process the data on Google's behalf. Google does not associate your IP address with any other data they hold.

Your Rights

You have the right to prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data in the future. This can be done by installing a browser add-on that disables Google Analytics. You can find this add-on at: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en.

For mobile device users or those who prefer not to install an add-on, there's an alternative method to opt out of Google Analytics data collection. By clicking on this link: Disable Google Analytics for this web site, you can install an "opt-out cookie" that prevents tracking on our website. This opt-out will remain active until the cookie is deleted from your browser.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
